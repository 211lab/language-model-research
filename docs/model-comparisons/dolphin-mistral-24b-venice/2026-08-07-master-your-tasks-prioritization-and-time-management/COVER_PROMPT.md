# Image Prompt
Create a vintage pulp illustration-style image with a 4:3 aspect ratio. The central subject is a focused individual sitting at a simple wooden desk, holding a pen over a notepad with three clearly written tasks: "Finish Report," "Client Call," and "Team Review." The desk has a single coffee mug placed prominently. The figure is dressed in classic 1950s attire, with a determined expression. Use a triangular composition with the figure at the apex, the desk as the base, and the coffee mug as a supporting element. Ensure the image has a visible halftone dot pattern, bold black linework, and cross-hatching for shading. Use a muted, earthy color palette with browns, beiges, and faded blues. Keep the background plain and solid-colored to emphasize the subject.

# Negative Prompt
Avoid including any modern technology, digital devices, or contemporary clothing. Do not use realistic environments, elaborate settings, or complex backgrounds. Exclude any symbols, icons, or typography. Avoid incorporating worksheets, paper artifacts, or document-like objects as the centerpiece. Do not include multiple figures or crowded environments. Exclude any photographic elements or modern photorealism.
