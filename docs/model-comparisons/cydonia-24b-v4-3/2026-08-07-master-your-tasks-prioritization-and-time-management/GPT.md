# Master Your Tasks Prioritization and Time Management

Welcome to the CLEAR system. This is your guide to taking control of your time and actually completing what matters most. Let's get started.

## Step 1: Capture

**Attention:** Your to-do list is winning. Every day you wake up with good intentions, but by evening you're wondering where the time went.

**Interest:** The problem isn't that you don't have enough time. The problem is you're trying to hold everything in your head, and your brain wasn't designed to be a to-do list.

**Desire:** Imagine starting your day with complete clarity about what actually matters. No more mental clutter. No more forgotten tasks. Just a clean list of everything that needs your attention.

**Action:** Right now, write down every single task, idea, and commitment floating in your head. Don't judge or organize yet. Just get it all out. Set a timer for 5 minutes and write without stopping. Include everything—work projects, personal errands, emails to send, calls to make, even tiny tasks like "buy coffee filters."
