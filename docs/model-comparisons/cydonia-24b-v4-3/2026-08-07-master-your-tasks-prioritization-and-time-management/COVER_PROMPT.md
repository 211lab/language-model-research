# Asset

## Image Prompt

Create a vintage pulp illustration in the style of 1940s-1950s comic books and illustrated advertisements. The scene depicts a focused individual at a workbench, but instead of the coffee mug mentioned in the text, the central subject is a **heavy iron anvil** with a glowing hot piece of metal resting upon it. The figure—a determined craftsman or blacksmith—is in the decisive moment of raising a hammer overhead, preparing to strike the metal with precision and force.

**Composition:**
- Aspect ratio: 4:3 (1536px x 1152px)
- Single focal point: the anvil with glowing metal
- Triangular arrangement: hammer (top), anvil (center), workbench surface (bottom)
- Dynamic diagonal composition with tension and movement
- Plain, solid-colored background (warm beige or faded brown)
- Minimal environmental detail—only what's necessary for scene comprehension

**Style:**
- Clean, object-centered vintage pulp illustration
- Visible halftone dot pattern for printed look
- Bold black linework with thick, prominent outlines
- Cross-hatching and ink-based shading for depth
- Limited, muted palette: earthy browns, beiges, warm grays, ochres
- Aged, printed, hand-illustrated, nostalgic feeling
- Dynamic composition with tension and impact

**Lighting:**
- Bright, readable lighting focused on the anvil and metal
- One clear center of brightness
- Most of the image in midtones
- Subject fully visible

**Subject:**
- Single figure: determined craftsman/blacksmith
- Simple, readable, fully subordinate to the clarity of the central action
- Decisive moment: hammer raised overhead, about to strike
- Emphasis on invention, action, readiness, pressure
- No worksheet, paper, document, or form elements
- No words, captions, or typography

**Technical:**
- No photography
- No modern photorealism
- No neon cyberpunk aesthetics
- No heavy noir mood
- No intricate engraving effects
- No realistic environments
- No UI mockups

## Negative Prompt

Avoid:
- Worksheets, paper artifacts, documents, blueprints, floating forms
- Photorealistic rendering
- Modern digital aesthetics
- Cyberpunk or neon elements
- Heavy noir atmosphere
- Intricate engraving effects
- Realistic environments with complex details
- UI mockups or screens
- Symbolic clutter or abstract concepts
- Multiple competing focal points
- Crowded compositions
- Elaborate settings or scenic vistas
- Photography or photorealistic elements
- Any text, captions, or typography
- Download buttons or CTA elements
- Worksheet-style layouts or document-centric designs

## Required On-Image Copy

There should be no on-image copy, text, captions, or typography of any kind on the final image.
