**What you're expected to do with the worksheet:**

Work through each section in order. Start tonight with a brain dump — write down every task, errand, and idea in your head. Then filter ruthlessly. Pick your three A-tasks. Order them by impact, not urgency. Carve out protected time blocks. Commit to single-tasking. At the end of each day, review what got done and what didn't. Carry over or cut. Repeat for five out of seven days.

The worksheet is not a form you fill out and forget. It's the mechanism that turns your intention into execution. It's the blower that regulates your fire.
