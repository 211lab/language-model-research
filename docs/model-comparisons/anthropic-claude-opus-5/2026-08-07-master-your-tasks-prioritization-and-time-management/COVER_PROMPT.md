# Image Prompt

Clean, object-centered vintage pulp illustration in the style of 1940s–1950s pulp magazine covers, comic book interiors, and mid-century illustrated advertisements. Aspect ratio `4:3`, horizontal.

**Central subject and decisive action:** A foundry worker mid-pour, tipping a single glowing crucible of molten metal so the bright stream lands squarely inside one narrow channel cut into the floor. This is the exact instant of routing — the heat is committed to one path. The stream is a clean, unbroken ribbon of light, not a splash. Show only one channel receiving the pour; the pour is decisive, controlled, aimed. The figure leans into the tilt with braced arms and a heavy glove grip, body angled with visible effort and forward momentum, communicating readiness and pressure under control. The worker's face is simple, stylized, and secondary — the eye must go to the crucible lip and the glowing stream first. No spill, no chaos, no scattered leaks.

**Composition:** Simple triangular arrangement — the worker's head and shoulders at the upper triangle point, the tilted crucible and pouring stream forming the descending edge, and the low floor channel acting as the flat support base. The brightest, highest-contrast area sits at the true center of the frame where the molten stream meets the channel opening. Strong subject separation: bold silhouette of figure and crucible read cleanly against a plain, solid, flat background field of warm gray-beige with no scenery, no walls, no machinery, no crowd, no architectural detail. Only the minimum floor plane needed for the channel to make sense. No competing focal points anywhere in the frame. Any heat shimmer or wisp of vapor is reduced to two or three simple stylized curls, flat and graphic, never atmospheric or smoky.

**Rendering style:** Bold black linework with thick, prominent, confident outlines. Cross-hatching and ink-based shading for volume in the sleeves, gloves, apron, crucible body, and floor edge. Visible halftone dot pattern across the flats for an aged printed look, with slight ink misregistration and paper grain. Hand-illustrated, nostalgic, printed-poster feeling rather than modern, digital, or painterly.

**Palette:** Limited, muted, slightly desaturated — earthy browns, beige, faded blue, warm grays, ochre, and one restrained red accent. The glow uses warm ochre and pale cream rather than saturated neon. Overall image sits in bright, readable midtones with one clear center of brightness; no deep black pits, no gloom, the subject fully visible and legible at small size.

**Mood:** Invention, readiness, disciplined force, impact. Dynamic tension and movement without noise.
