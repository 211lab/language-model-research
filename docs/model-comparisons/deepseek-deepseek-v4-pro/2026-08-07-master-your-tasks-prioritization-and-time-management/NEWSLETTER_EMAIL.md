The same three priorities sit on tomorrow’s list, heavier than they were this morning.

This is not a time problem. It’s a prioritization problem disguised as a time problem. You have enough hours. What you lack is a mechanism that forces your attention onto the right tasks and keeps it there until the work is done. This week, you install that mechanism.

Inside your packet, you’ll find the lesson (attached as a PDF), the public letter, and the GPT coach. But the center of your week is the worksheet. It’s not a collection of tips. It’s a forge. Your practical goal is simple and non-negotiable: complete your top three priorities before noon for five straight days—without extra hours, lost sleep, or ignoring your team. The worksheet will produce a daily chain of checked-off wins, a clear record of what pulled you off course, and a self-correcting loop that gets more accurate every day. This is where the Forge phase of your larger transformation becomes real. You stop scattering your force and start striking what matters.
