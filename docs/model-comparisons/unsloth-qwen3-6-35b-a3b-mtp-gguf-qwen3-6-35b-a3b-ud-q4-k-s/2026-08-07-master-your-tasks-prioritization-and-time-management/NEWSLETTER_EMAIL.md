Attached to this email is the PRIORITIZE Worksheet. This is the central execution artifact for the week. It is designed to force you to define success before you start working. You will fill this out once, with precision, to establish the structure for your next seven days.

Do not rush. Do not skim. Treat each letter as a command.

P - Pick Your Big Rocks. Start every morning by identifying the 2-3 most critical tasks that will have the biggest impact. Do not start with your inbox. Write them down first. These are your "Big Rocks." If you do nothing else today, these must be done.

R - Remove the Noise. Once your priorities are set, eliminate distractions. Silence your phone, close unrelated browser tabs, and inform others you are in focus mode. Create a barrier between you and the interruptions.

I - Implement in Blocks. Schedule specific time blocks for your Big Rocks. Use the Pomodoro technique (25 minutes work, 5 minutes break) to maintain intensity. Treat these blocks like unmissable appointments with yourself.

O - Organize the Rest. After completing your Big Rocks, handle the remaining tasks. Group similar small tasks together. Schedule these for lower-energy times in the day.

R - Review and Reset. At the end of the day, review what you accomplished. Check off completed tasks. Carry over unfinished items to tomorrow’s list, reprioritizing as needed.

I - Iterate Daily. Consistency is key. Repeat this process every single day. Adjust your priorities based on new information, but never skip the process.

T - Track Your Progress. Keep a simple log of your daily priorities and outcomes. Look for patterns. Are you consistently failing to complete Big Rocks? Why? Adjust your time estimates or environment.

O - Optimize Your Environment. Continuously improve your workspace and tools. Remove physical clutter. Use digital tools to capture tasks so nothing falls through the cracks.

Z - Zero Out the Day. End your day with a clear mind. Ensure your to-do list for tomorrow is ready. Do not leave loose ends that cause anxiety overnight.

This worksheet is where you build the container. Heat without structure wastes energy. If you let every notification, every request, and every distraction enter your space, you have no structure.
