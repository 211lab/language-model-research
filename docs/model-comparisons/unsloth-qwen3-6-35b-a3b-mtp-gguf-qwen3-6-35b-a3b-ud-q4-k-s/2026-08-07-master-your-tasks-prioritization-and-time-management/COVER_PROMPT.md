# Image Prompt
A 4:3 editorial cover illustration in a clean, object-centered vintage pulp style, referencing 1940s-1950s comic books and illustrated advertisements. The scene depicts the decisive moment of "The Morning Lock" from the long-form letter: a simple, readable male figure in a dark suit sits at a sturdy wooden workbench, his posture rigid and focused. He holds a heavy, black fountain pen, poised to write on a single sheet of paper. The central focal point is the tip of the pen touching the paper, rendered with the brightest glow and highest contrast in the composition, symbolizing the act of prioritization and taking command. The background is a plain, solid muted beige wall, minimally detailed to ensure subject separation. The lighting is bright and readable, prioritizing clarity over mood, with most of the image in midtones. The palette is limited and desaturated, using earthy browns, faded blues, warm grays, ochres, and restrained reds. Visible halftone dot patterns create a printed look, with bold black linework, thick prominent outlines, and cross-hatching for depth. The composition uses a simple triangular arrangement between the figure's head, the pen tip, and the workbench surface. No words, no typography, no labels. No modern photorealism, no neon, no cyberpunk, no gloomy noir.
