## Model
- **F - Fast Brain Dump:** Empty your head completely onto paper or a digital screen. Write down every single project, task, and errand so nothing stays trapped in your working memory.
- **I - Identify Big Rocks:** Pick the top two or three tasks that will create the biggest positive impact on your day. These are your non-negotiable priority items.
- **L - Label Urgency vs Importance:** Categorize the remaining items into secondary duties, tasks to pass to others, or items to postpone. Separate real urgency from simple noise.
- **T - Time Block Priorities:** Lock dedicated focus blocks into your calendar early in the day for your top priority tasks when your energy and willpower are highest.
- **E - Eliminate Distractions:** Turn off notifications, silence your phone, and close extra tabs during focus blocks to protect your attention until the core task is done.
- **R - Review and Reset Daily:** Check off finished items at the end of the day, assess what remains, and set up your next day's task list before you log off.

## Action Steps Summary
1. **Dump and Select:** Write down all your open tasks once a day, then pull out the top three items that move the needle most.
2. **Block and Protect:** Schedule dedicated focus time early in your day for your top three tasks, removing all digital interruptions while you work.
3. **Review and Carry Over:** End each day by checking finished work, clearing out junk tasks, and building tomorrow's clear action plan.

## FAQ
- **Why do I work hard all day but still feel like I got nothing done?**
  Because you are spending your best energy on small, urgent chores instead of high-value tasks. When you switch to prioritizing top items first, your daily effort translates into real progress.
- **How do I deal with constant interruptions from my boss or team?**
  Set specific time blocks for deep focus and communicate them clearly. Batch your replies and updates into set windows during the day so you remain helpful without losing focus.
- **What should I do if my task list keeps growing out of control?**
  Be brutal about cutting or delaying items that do not fit your primary goals. A long list creates panic, while a tight list of three daily priorities creates fast action.
- **What if emergency tasks pop up and ruin my plan for the day?**
  Leave room in your afternoon schedule for unexpected requests. Protect your morning focus blocks so your top priorities get completed even if the afternoon gets messy.
- **How do I know which three tasks are actually my top priorities?**
  Ask yourself which three items would make today a total success if nothing else got done. Focus on actions that create long-term momentum, not just noisy requests.
