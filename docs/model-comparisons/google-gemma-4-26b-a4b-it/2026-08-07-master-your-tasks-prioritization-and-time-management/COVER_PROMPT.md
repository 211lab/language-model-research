# Image Prompt

A vintage 1950s pulp illustration of a decisive moment: a heavy, solid stone "Big Rock" being placed firmly into the center of a glowing, industrial furnace grate. The scene captures the tension of containment and order. The central focus is the massive, textured stone making contact with the intense, bright orange heat of the furnace interior. A simple, strong hand is visible at the edge of the frame, guiding the stone with purpose. The composition follows a strict triangular arrangement between the heavy stone, the glowing furnace opening, and the dark metal base. The style features bold black outlines, heavy ink-based cross-hatching for shadows, and a visible halftone dot pattern. The color palette is limited to muted ochre, deep charcoal, warm grays, and a single concentrated area of bright, desaturated orange-red at the center of the heat. The background is a solid, dark, minimally detailed industrial gray to ensure the central action pops. 1536px x 1152px, 4:3 aspect ratio.
