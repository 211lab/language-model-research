## Post

You wake up with a plan, but by noon, your day’s been hijacked. The inbox is a battlefield, your phone won’t stop, and your to-do list mutates faster than you can cross things off. You end up exhausted, but the work that actually matters? Still untouched. Tomorrow, you promise. But tomorrow is just another rerun.

This is the cycle that keeps men stuck: urgent distractions steal your focus, and the important work gets buried. You’re not short on effort—you’re short on clarity and control. If you don’t choose your priorities, the world will choose for you. That’s how your time, energy, and direction get siphoned away, day after day.

This week, we’re breaking that cycle. The lesson and lecture will show you how to filter your tasks, defend your priorities, and finally move the needle on what matters most. But the real shift happens when you put this into practice—when you use the worksheet.

The worksheet is your tool for the week. It’s not just another list; it’s the filter that cuts through the noise and puts you back in control. You’ll use it to dump every task, pick your top three, block time, and defend it—so you end each day with real wins, not just boxes checked.

Don’t wait until the group meets to see how this works. The worksheet is your entry ticket to the conversation. Bring your real results, your friction, your questions. The group discussion will go deeper, but it starts with you doing the work—filtering, blocking, and executing—before we meet.

This is the week you stop letting your day get hijacked. Grab the worksheet, run the process, and come ready to talk about what changed. Your progress starts now.
