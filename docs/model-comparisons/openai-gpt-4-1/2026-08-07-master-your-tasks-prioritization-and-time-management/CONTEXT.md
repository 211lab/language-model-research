## Actionable VERB
FILTER

## Model

**F**ocus First: Start each day by listing every task on your mind. Then, ask yourself: “If I only get three things done today, which will make the biggest difference?” Mark these as your top priorities.

**I**dentify Importance: Use a simple system (like the Eisenhower Matrix) to sort tasks by what’s urgent and what’s truly important. Move the most important tasks to the top of your list.

**L**ock Time: Block out time on your calendar for your top 3 tasks. Treat these blocks like appointments you can’t miss. Schedule them when your energy is highest.

**T**une Out Distractions: When working on a priority, silence your phone, close extra tabs, and let others know you’re in focus mode. Use a timer (like Pomodoro: 25 minutes work, 5 minutes break) to stay on track.

**E**xecute Relentlessly: Work on your top tasks until they’re done or your time block is up. Don’t let less important chores steal your focus.

**R**eview and Reprioritize: At the end of the day, check off what you finished. Move unfinished tasks to tomorrow’s list, and ask if they’re still important. Adjust your plan as needed to keep moving forward.

## Action Steps Summary

1. **Pick Your Priorities:** Brain-dump your tasks and choose the top 3 that matter most for today.
2. **Block and Defend Your Time:** Schedule focused work blocks for your priorities and protect them from distractions.
3. **Review and Reset:** At day’s end, check off wins, move unfinished tasks, and adjust your plan for tomorrow.

## FAQ

**1. Why do I always feel busy but never finish what matters?**  
Because urgent tasks and distractions crowd out your real priorities. FILTER helps you focus on what counts first.

**2. How do I know what’s actually important?**  
Ask: “If I only did three things today, which would make the biggest difference?” Use tools like the Eisenhower Matrix to sort tasks.

**3. What if I can’t finish my top tasks every day?**  
That’s normal. Review what got in the way, move unfinished tasks to tomorrow, and adjust your plan. Progress beats perfection.

**4. How do I handle interruptions from others?**  
Let people know when you’re in focus mode. Silence notifications and set boundaries during your priority work blocks.

**5. Isn’t it selfish to say no or delegate?**  
No. Prioritizing your most important work helps you deliver better results for everyone—including yourself.
