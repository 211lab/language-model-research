Here’s what matters most: the worksheet.

This is not a generic planner. It is the central execution artifact for the week. Everything else—lesson, letter, GPT—exists to support how you use it.

Why this worksheet, why now:
- You’re drowning in tasks, interruptions, and “urgent” noise.
- You want to get ahead at work without working later.
- You want steady progress on long-term goals.
- You want less last-minute stress and more time for family, health, and rest.

None of that happens if you keep letting noise run your day.

The worksheet walks you through the FOCUS model:
- F – Find your real tasks
- O – Order by impact
- C – Choose your big rocks
- U – Use time blocks
- S – Shield your attention

Use it daily for the next 7 days.

What you’re expected to do:
- Each morning, open the worksheet.
- Do a complete brain dump of all tasks (work, home, personal).
- Label each task A, B, or C by impact.
- Choose 2–3 “big rocks” (A tasks) that truly move you forward.
- Schedule them as real time blocks.
- Define how you’ll protect your focus during those blocks.
- At the end of the day, review what you completed and adjust tomorrow.

This is how your Furnace Stack gets rebuilt:
- You create a single source of truth for your tasks.
- You prioritize by impact, not noise.
- You protect time for the work that actually matters.

If you do this for 7 days:
- You will complete more of your most important tasks.
- You will reduce time on low-value busywork.
- You will finish your workday earlier, without dropping key responsibilities.

If you don’t:
- You keep the same scattered routine.
- You keep feeling busy but stuck.
- You keep letting noise decide your priorities.
