## Subject

Your Week Starts Tonight: Pick Three, Guard the Block, Finish What Counts

## Body

You worked all day. You answered messages, attended meetings, cleared small requests. By five o'clock you were tired. But the one task that actually mattered—the one that would move something forward, the one someone is waiting on—sat untouched at the bottom of your list.

That is not a discipline problem. It is a targeting problem. Your energy went everywhere and landed nowhere. The heat was real. The direction was missing.

This week fixes that.

**What is inside your packet:**

- **The worksheet** (attached as PDF) — your working tool for the week
- **The lesson** (attached as PDF) — the full breakdown of why your days keep slipping and what FILTER does about it
- **The weekly essay** (linked below) — a longer look at the pattern of loud tasks beating quiet ones, and how to reverse it

**Your job this week is the worksheet.**

The worksheet walks you through FILTER, a six-step sequence that moves you from a scattered, overloaded mind to a written, prioritized plan you can execute and close. Each section asks you to produce something specific:

1. **Fetch** every task out of your head and onto one list.
2. **Isolate** your top three—the tasks that create the biggest impact if finished today.
3. **Limit** the urgent noise by delegating, delaying, batching, or removing what does not belong.
4. **Time-block** your top three into your calendar during your best energy hours.
5. **Execute** in order, one task at a time, with alerts silenced and tabs closed.
6. **Review** what you finished, cut what no longer matters, and write tomorrow's top three before you stop.

The worksheet is not a reading exercise. It is a building exercise. You fill it out, you run the cycle, and by the end of five days you have proof that your top three can be done before the workday closes—without working late, without skipping breaks, without answering every request the moment it arrives.

**Where this fits in your larger system:**

You are building a daily structure that protects your energy and directs it toward results. This worksheet is the mechanism that turns intention into execution. Without it, your plan lives in your head and dissolves by noon. With it, your plan lives on paper, has a time slot, and has a review that closes the loop. Each day you run FILTER, you are training yourself to decide what matters before the day decides for you. That is the shift from reactive to deliberate. That is where real progress starts.

**Your challenge this week:**

Run the FILTER cycle daily for five days. Each morning, start with your top three already chosen. Each evening, close with a review and tomorrow's targets written down. Five days. Three tasks done before the day ends. No exceptions.

You already have the energy. You already have the desire. What you have been missing is a place to aim it. The worksheet gives you that place. Fill it out tonight. Pick your three. Guard the block tomorrow morning. Strike until the metal moves.
