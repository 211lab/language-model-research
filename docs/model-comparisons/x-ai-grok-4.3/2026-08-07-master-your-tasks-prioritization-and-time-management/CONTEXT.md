## Title
Stop Letting Busy Work Steal Your Best Hours

## Subtitle
Learn to pick the three tasks that move your goals forward and get them done before the day takes over.

## Promise
Finish your top three tasks by 2 p.m. on four out of five workdays while keeping the same total hours.

## Motivations
- Finish important work without staying late
- Know exactly what to do first each morning
- Cut the number of half-done tasks on your list
- Spend less time on email and quick requests
- End the day with a short list of finished items instead of a long list of leftovers

## Challenges & Sacrifices
- New messages and meetings push planned work to later
- Every task feels urgent once it lands in your inbox
- You keep three or four big projects open at once
- Small errands eat the first hour of the day
- You say yes to extra work before checking your own list

## Frustrations
- You start the day with a long list and finish almost none of it
- Important projects keep getting pushed to tomorrow
- You feel busy all day but cannot name three things you finished
- You check email first and lose the next two hours
- You keep rewriting the same to-do list without making progress

## Transformations
- From rewriting the same list every morning to finishing the top three items before lunch
- From answering every message right away to answering only after the main tasks are done
- From juggling five open projects to closing one before opening the next
- From working late to catch up to leaving on time with the key work complete
- From feeling pulled in every direction to following a clear order each day

## Choice
Keep reacting to whatever shows up first and stay behind on the work that matters, or start each day by naming the three tasks that count and finish them before anything else.

## Actionable VERB
DIRECT

## Model
D – Dump every task onto one list first thing so nothing stays in your head.  
I – Identify the three tasks that will move your main goals if nothing else gets done.  
R – Rank the rest as B or C so you know what can wait.  
E – Execute the top three in order during your highest-energy hours.  
C – Close the door on new requests until the A tasks are finished.  
T – Track what got done at the end of the day and move only the unfinished A tasks forward.

## Action Steps Summary
- Pick Three: Write every task down, then circle the three that matter most today.  
- Block First: Put those three into your calendar before 2 p.m. and protect the time.  
- Review Fast: At day’s end check off what finished and carry only the real leftovers to tomorrow.

## FAQ
- Why does my list keep growing even when I work all day? You add new tasks faster than you finish the old ones because you have no daily cutoff.  
- How do I stop email from taking the whole morning? Set a rule that email only opens after the first A task is done.  
- What if everything on my list feels important? Ask which three items would still matter next week; the rest drop to B or C.  
- How do I handle a boss who adds tasks all day? Write the new task on the list, then ask which of the current A tasks should move down.  
- Why do I still feel behind even after a full day? You finish many small items but skip the few that actually move your goals.
