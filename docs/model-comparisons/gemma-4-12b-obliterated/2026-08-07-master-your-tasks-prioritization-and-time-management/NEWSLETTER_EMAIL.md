## Subject
Stop Firefighting: Master Your Task Hierarchy

## Body

Your daily workflow should be a planned construction project, not a constant exercise in firefighting.

When every task on a list is treated as equally important, the list becomes a source of chaos rather than a roadmap for progress. This leads to constant context switching—the mental tax of jumping between different types of tasks—which exhausts your focus and fragments your ability to execute. To move from a reactive posture to a proactive one, you must establish a clear hierarchy where the "Big Rocks" are prioritized over the minor errands that often consume the most available time.

This week, you are working toward building a controlled and efficient workflow. By establishing a structured method to prioritize, you ensure that the energy available for work is directed toward the correct objectives. This is a critical step in transforming a chaotic environment into a structured system where progress is intentionally shaped.

To complete this week's objectives, you are provided with a comprehensive set of resources:

*   **The Worksheet:** This is your primary execution artifact for the week. You will use it to move from a reactive state to a proactive, structured execution model by identifying, prioritizing, and executing high-impact tasks.
*   **The Lesson:** This provides the foundational knowledge needed to understand why task hierarchy is a structural requirement and how to identify the "Big Rocks."
*   **The GPT Coach:** Use the GPT to get the most out of the worksheet. It acts as a guide to help you navigate the complexities of sorting, targeting, and isolating high-priority tasks.
*   **The Public Letter:** This provides additional context on the importance of building a structured workflow to ensure that work is not just constant, but purposeful.

By completing the worksheet, you will develop a clear roadmap for your day, allowing you to protect your time from being hijacked by lower-priority items. This process will enable you to move from a state of being overwhelmed by a massive list of tasks to having a clear, actionable path toward your goals.

You are now equipped to move from a reactive state to a proactive, structured execution model.

Build your hierarchy. Master your tasks.
