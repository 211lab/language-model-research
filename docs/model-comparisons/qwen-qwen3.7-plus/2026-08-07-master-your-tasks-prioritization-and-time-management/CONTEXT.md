## Title
Finish Your Most Important Work Every Single Day

## Subtitle
Stop letting urgent but useless tasks steal your time. Learn how to pick the right work, block out distractions, and actually get your big goals done.

## Promise
Complete your top 3 most important tasks every day for the next 7 days without working extra hours, skipping meals, or giving up your weekends.

## Actionable VERB
FILTER

## Model
**F - Find:** Write down every single thing you need to do. Get it all out of your head and onto paper or a screen so nothing falls through the cracks.

**I - Isolate:** Look at your master list and pick your top 3 big rocks. These are the specific tasks that will make the biggest positive impact today. 

**L - Lock:** Block out time on your calendar for these 3 tasks. Put them early in the day when your energy is high, and treat this time like a doctor's appointment you cannot miss.

**T - Tame:** Kill your distractions. Put your phone in another room, close extra browser tabs, and tell your team you are in focus mode. 

**E - Execute:** Do the work. Work in focused sprints of 25 to 50 minutes. Do not stop until the time block is up or the task is completely done.

**R - Review:** Check off what you finished to get a quick win. Move unfinished tasks to tomorrow, and pick your new top 3 tasks for the next day.

## Action Steps Summary
1. **Filter the Noise:** Brain dump all your tasks and isolate your top 3 most important goals for the day so you know exactly what matters.
2. **Block the Time:** Lock in focused work sessions on your calendar for your top tasks and tame all distractions to protect your energy.
3. **Review the Wins:** Execute your focused work, check off your daily wins, and review your list to set up a clear plan for tomorrow.

## FAQ
**Why do I feel so tired at the end of the day even when I didn't do much?**
You are switching tasks too often. Jumping between emails, chats, and projects drains your brain. Focus on one big task at a time to save your mental energy.

**How do I stop saying yes to every little favor my coworkers ask for?**
You need a strict prioritization rule. If a new request does not beat your top 3 tasks for the day, it goes on a list for later or gets a polite no.

**Why does my to-do list never get any shorter?**
You are adding easy chores to feel busy instead of tackling hard, important work. Pick three big tasks and ignore the rest until they are done.

**How can I focus when my phone keeps buzzing and people keep interrupting me?**
You have to physically block the distractions. Put your phone in another room, close your email, and tell people you are in focus mode for the next hour.

**What do I do when an emergency ruins my whole schedule?**
Not everything is a real emergency. Ask yourself if it will matter next week. If not, schedule it for later and protect your top tasks first.
