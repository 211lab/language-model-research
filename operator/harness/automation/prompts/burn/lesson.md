CORE PROGRAM RULE
Each piece must do three things:
1) Teach one practical, usable truth.
2) Give that truth a clear place inside a larger system.
3) Translate pressure into action, and action into identity.
The system is not the lesson. It is the container that makes the lesson executable.

CORE CONTENT PHILOSOPHY
Value First:
- Start with a grounded life lesson.
- Use concrete examples, arguments, or metaphor.
- Address a real problem the reader recognizes.
- Do not introduce the system until the problem is clear.

System as Sense-Maker:
- Introduce the system only after the problem is established.
- Use it to explain root causes, not surface symptoms.
- Use it to organize pressure, effort, and growth.

Single Component Focus:
- Anchor the entire piece inside ONE component.
- Never blend components.
- The chosen component is the memory hook and the mechanical explanation.

Action Closure:
- End with a direct challenge.
- No comfort. No reassurance.
- Leave the reader with a clear responsibility to act or remain unchanged.

THE SYSTEM CONTEXT
The 3F system has 3 phases, 9 components each (27 total):
Phase One: The Furnace (build or strengthen your Furnace)
- Converts emotional pressure into usable energy.
Phase Two: The Forge (living in the Forge)
- Turns energy into skill and identity.
Phase Three: Forms (blades, domains of mastery, the outward application)
- Applies identity to the external world.

Each lesson must live fully inside one component and use its function to explain:
- what is breaking
- why it breaks
- what function must be restored

PHASE ONE: FURNACE COMPONENTS (choose ONE)
1. Furnace Stack (roles and responsibilities): roles give pressure a path, no roles means effort leaks.
2. Charge (what you consume and believe): inputs shape output, bad inputs weaken drive.
3. Hearth (emotional core): longing, rage, desire, purpose, if cold, effort has no endurance.
4. Ports/Tuyeres (environment and pressures): mentors, friction, challenge, without pressure growth stalls.
5. Blower System (discipline): consistent action keeps the fire active, without it nothing combusts.
6. Refractory Lining (values and boundaries): prevents collapse and self-destruction.
7. Slag Channel (emotional release): clears resentment and distorted stories, waste poisons output.
8. Tap (raw self-expression): safe outlet for pressure, suppression becomes internal damage.
9. Cast House Run (insight to action): turns realization into immediate behavior, without it insight stays theory.
Usage rule: Explain the component's function, diagnose failure, show how restoring it converts pressure into usable energy. No blending.

PHASE TWO: FORGE COMPONENTS (choose ONE)
1. Casting Ladle (hold emotion and insight): reflection and rest, without it intensity becomes overwhelm.
2. Anvil (structure for growth): habit and stability, no stable surface means no shaping.
3. Hammer (focused reps): deliberate practice, random effort is noise, focused blows create change.
4. Quench (testing under stress): action with stakes, without it growth stays soft.
5. Tempering Fire (recalibration and self-compassion): keeps strength flexible, prevents brittleness and burnout.
6. Tongs (regulation tools): support, journaling, breathwork, guidance, tools prevent heat from becoming damage.
7. Grindstone (refinement): feedback and precision, removes roughness, makes effort clean.
8. Stress Test (real-world proof): relationships, failure, leadership, what holds becomes reliable.
9. Blade (a forged dimension of identity): one specific trait, role, or capability (discipline, leadership, craft). Not the whole identity. Proven only through use.
Usage rule: Explain the component's function, diagnose failure, show how restoring it turns energy into skill and identity. No blending.
