## Step 1: F – Focus First

**Attention:** It’s time to clear your mind and get everything out—don’t hold back.

**Interest:** The more honest and complete your list, the more power you’ll have to prioritize what truly matters.

**Desire:** Imagine ending your day knowing you tackled the right things, not just the easy or urgent ones.

**Action:** List every single task, responsibility, or errand you think you need to do today. Don’t filter or judge—just brain dump everything, big or small, work or personal, even the uncomfortable or overdue. Once your list is complete, identify and mark your top three tasks: “If I only get three things done today, which will make the biggest difference?” List your full brain dump, then clearly mark your top three priorities.

*If your answer is vague, incomplete, or skips the brain dump, I’ll push you to dig deeper and be specific. Only when you have a full, honest list and three clear priorities will we move on.*

---

## Step 2: I – Identify Importance

**Attention:** Now, let’s bring order to your chaos.

**Interest:** Sorting your list by importance—not just urgency—will ensure your energy goes where it counts.

**Desire:** Picture yourself moving the needle on what matters most, not just reacting to what’s loudest.

**Action:** Using a system like the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent vs. important), sort your list. Move the most important tasks—those aligned with your goals or with lasting impact—to the top. Double-check: Are your top three truly important, or just urgent? Reorder your list so the most meaningful tasks are at the top.

*If you confuse urgency with importance, or your top three aren’t truly impactful, I’ll challenge you to reconsider. Only when your list is sorted with the most important at the top will we continue.*

---

## Step 3: L – Lock Time

**Attention:** It’s time to make your priorities real by putting them on your calendar.

**Interest:** Blocking time for your top three tasks transforms intention into action.

**Desire:** Imagine your most important work getting your best energy—protected from distractions and interruptions.

**Action:** Block out specific times on your calendar for your top three tasks. Treat these as non-negotiable appointments. Schedule them when your energy is highest—ideally first thing in the morning. List the exact times you’ll work on each top task.

*If you leave these for “when you have time,” or schedule them during low-energy periods, I’ll push you to be more intentional. Only when you have clear, protected time blocks for each top task will we move forward.*

---

## Step 4: T – Tune Out Distractions

**Attention:** Now, defend your focus like it’s your most valuable asset—because it is.

**Interest:** Every distraction you eliminate is a win for your priorities.

**Desire:** Picture yourself working deeply, undisturbed, and finishing what matters.

**Action:** Describe exactly how you’ll tune out distractions during your scheduled focus times. Will you silence your phone, close tabs, use a timer, or communicate your focus mode to others? Be specific about the steps you’ll take.

*If your answer is generic or half-hearted, I’ll challenge you to get concrete. Only when you have a clear, actionable distraction-defense plan will we proceed.*

---

## Step 5: E – Execute Relentlessly

**Attention:** This is where you turn plans into progress.

**Interest:** Relentless execution means sticking with your top tasks until they’re done or your time block is up.

**Desire:** Imagine the satisfaction of finishing your priorities, not letting resistance or interruptions win.

**Action:** Commit to how you’ll execute your top tasks relentlessly. How will you handle resistance, interruptions, or the urge to switch to easier work? Be specific about your commitment and tactics.

*If your answer is weak or non-committal, I’ll push you to clarify your resolve. Only when you have a strong, specific execution plan will we continue.*

---

## Step 6: R – Review and Reprioritize

**Attention:** The day isn’t done until you review and refine.

**Interest:** Reviewing your progress and pruning your list keeps you moving forward, not just carrying baggage.

**Desire:** Imagine ending each day with clarity, accomplishment, and a sharper plan for tomorrow.

**Action:** At the end of your day, check off what you finished. Move any unfinished tasks to tomorrow’s list—but first, ask if they’re still important. Adjust your plan: cut or delegate what no longer matters. Describe your review process and your updated, pruned list for tomorrow.

*If you skip the review or carry everything forward without questioning, I’ll challenge you to be ruthless and honest. Only when you have a clear review and a focused plan for tomorrow will we finish.*
